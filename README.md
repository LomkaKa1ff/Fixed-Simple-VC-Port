## About
When using Simple Voice Chat mod, port for Voice Chat is generated randomly every time you open LAN connection for your Single Player world. This mod lets you choose your own Voice Chat port that will be saved in config file of the mod.

## How to use
For choosing your own Voice Chat port you need to open the **Open to LAN** menu and then you will see a new **Voice Chat Port** field. Default port is **24454**, but you can choose your own port between **1-65535**.

![Image of choosing own Voice Chat port in mod Fixed Simple VC Port](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/2502691f9456095ca57019c99451ce09398a30fe.png)

After choosing your port you need to click a **Start LAN World** and in chat you will see your current Voice Chat port under world port.

![Image of chat message of a current selected port for Voice Chat in mod Fixed Simple VC Port](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/91e9ac4599bedae1f564e14e318cfce4407a72d4_0.webp)

Your selected Voice Chat port will be saved in config file of the mod and it will be updated when you choose other port in **Open to LAN** menu.

Make sure you did port forwarding in your router for your selected port for Single Player World and Voice Chat port. Port for opening your Single Player World port needs TCP protocol and Voice Chat port needs UPD type of protocol.

## Installation Requirements
Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge, Simple Voice Chat 2.6.22.

This mod can use only host for your Single Player World and other players don't need to download this mod.
